# Candidate historical forecast and renewable sources

Investigated for later ML milestones; **none of these feeds is integrated into the January realised-history pipeline**. An archive value with a *valid time* is not necessarily information that was available at that time. Forecast features need the issuing run and publication/availability timestamp.

| Source | Availability and format | Publication timing and limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [AEMO aggregated price and demand](https://www.aemo.com.au/energy-systems/electricity/national-electricity-market-nem/data-nem/aggregated-data) | Monthly per-region CSV on NEMWeb; NSW1 `RRP` in AUD/MWh and `TOTALDEMAND` in MW, five-minute rows for the current era. | Realised historical outcomes. AEMO says prices can be revised through the next business day and are final on the second business day of the following month. Monthly CSVs are unsuitable as decision-time price features. |
| [AEMO public dispatch](https://www.aemo.com.au/energy-systems/electricity/national-electricity-market-nem/data-nem/market-management-system-mms-data/dispatch) | Five-minute regional dispatch CSVs and daily archives include price, demand and other dispatch quantities. | More detailed, run-specific format than monthly aggregates; historical archive access and publication lag should be checked before using any field as a feature. |
| [AEMO generation and load](https://www.aemo.com.au/energy-systems/electricity/national-electricity-market-nem/data-nem/market-management-system-mms-data/generation-and-load) | `public_dispatchSCADA_*.csv` has five-minute unit `SCADAVALUE` in MW; older files move to monthly archives. Unit-to-fuel classification is needed to aggregate NSW wind/solar, and unit status can change. | SCADA sampled actuals, not forecasts. AEMO notes some `MWH_READING` values in non-scheduled generation files are **instantaneous MW despite their name**. Do not sum them as MWh without conversion. Rooftop PV is separate from utility-scale SCADA. |
| [AEMO rooftop PV actuals and forecasts](https://www.aemo.com.au/energy-systems/electricity/national-electricity-market-nem/nem-forecasting-and-planning/operational-forecasting/solar-and-wind-energy-forecasting/australian-solar-energy-forecasting-system) | Regional estimated actuals and forecasts in separate CSV reports; historical rooftop actuals are half-hourly and measured/satellite estimates, not household metering. | AEMO describes actual estimates as every 30 minutes with roughly 30-minute delay. Forecast products cover different lead times. Historical coverage and exact issue times require a separate archive audit before integration. |
| [AEMO P5MIN and predispatch](https://tech-specs.docs.public.aemo.com.au/Content/TSP_EMMSDM55_Apr2025/EMMS_DMv5.5_Apr25_1.htm) | Public P5MIN results contain 12 five-minute forecast periods for the next hour, updated every five minutes; 30-minute predispatch extends into the next market day. Report archives are zipped AEMO/MMS CSV and much larger than the monthly price files. | Must retain `RUN_DATETIME` as the issuance key, target interval, region, and actual publication time. [AEMO's Project EDGE specification](https://www.aemo.com.au/-/media/files/initiatives/der/2022/edge-data-specs-part-b.pdf) describes P5MIN as normally published around 40 seconds into an interval, with the first period already containing that interval's actual spot price and the next eleven being forecasts. A later run for the same target would leak future information. Exact archive coverage and delivery delay for 2023–26 have **not** been validated in this milestone. |
| [Open-Meteo Historical Forecast API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-forecast-api) | Continuous hourly model forecast history, typically JSON/CSV; coverage starts around 2021 depending on model. | Stitches early portions of successive runs and does not by itself preserve a full issued forecast horizon. Model versions change over time. Do not treat its valid-time series as a known-ahead forecast. |
| [Open-Meteo Previous Runs API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/previous-runs-api) | JSON/CSV variables at fixed lead times of 0–7 days; most models archived from January 2024, with exceptions such as GFS temperature from 2021. | Suitable for selected lead-time skill tests, but cannot cover every chosen variable/model in the 2023 training window without checking availability. |
| [Open-Meteo Single Runs API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-forecast-api) | Full original forecast runs selected by UTC initialisation time; ECMWF IFS HRES from March 2024, other model runs mostly from April 2026 per current docs. | Run initialisation is not the same as API availability. The model-update metadata reports availability times for current runs, but historical availability needs validation. Coverage is insufficient for a uniform 2023–26 issued-forecast dataset using one model. |

For the current realised-history table, [Open-Meteo Historical Weather API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api) with `models=era5` provides hourly *reanalysis* in UTC. ERA5 covers the whole planned period; it is a historical estimate of conditions, not the forecast the agent would have seen. Its `shortwave_radiation` value is the mean over the **preceding** hour in W/m². The exact request URL and downloaded bytes are recorded in `raw/source_manifest.json`.

## AEMO forecast follow-up — November 2026

The earlier P5MIN row above was preliminary. The completed [January 2025 source and ingestion audit](AEMO_FORECAST_FEASIBILITY.md) supersedes its unvalidated archive-coverage statement. Official MMSDM indexes contain `P5MIN_REGIONSOLUTION`, `PREDISPATCHPRICE`, and `PREDISPATCHREGIONSUM` files for all 35 months January 2023–November 2025. January P5MIN preserves run vintages; the two monthly 30-minute tables contain only one row per target half hour. Exact public release timestamps are absent from the downloaded monthly tables. For the existing exact `t+60` five-minute target, P5MIN coverage is zero on the 576-decision smoke sample under all tested publication-delay assumptions; no forecast accuracy score is claimed.

### Complete predispatch archive follow-up

[NEMSEER's glossary](https://nemseer.readthedocs.io/en/stable/glossary.html) distinguishes the ordinary monthly `DATA/` tables, which hold only the latest predispatch forecast per target, from the separate [`PREDISP_ALL_DATA/` complete archive](https://nemweb.com.au/Data_Archive/Wholesale_Electricity/MMSDM/2025/MMSDM_2025_01/MMSDM_Historical_Data_SQLLoader/PREDISP_ALL_DATA/). [Its source code](https://github.com/UNSW-CEEM/NEMSEER/blob/master/src/nemseer/data.py) routes complete `PRICE`, `REGIONSUM` and related tables there. Direct index checks found both complete price and regional-summary files in **all 35 monthly directories from January 2023 through November 2025**; only January 2025 contents were downloaded. The complete January files contain **82,584 NSW1 rows across 1,488 run identifiers** for each table, whereas the corresponding `DATA/` tables have only 1,488 NSW1 rows. The separate complete archive resolves the vintage-history limitation for 30-minute forecasts. Details and matched January diagnostics are in [AEMO_HORIZON_RESULTS.md](AEMO_HORIZON_RESULTS.md).

[AEMO's price-table schema](https://visualisations.aemo.com.au/aemo/nemweb/MMSDataModelReport/Electricity/MMS%20Data%20Model%20Report_files/MMS_276.htm) defines `PREDISPATCHSEQNO` as a nominal `YYYYMMDDPP` run key with `01` at 04:30 fixed AEST, `PERIODID` as a count, `RUNNO` as a rerun number and `LASTCHANGED` as the time the record was last updated. [NEMSEER parses the sequence](https://github.com/UNSW-CEEM/NEMSEER/blob/master/src/nemseer/data_handlers.py) into nominal run time and describes `LASTCHANGED` as often close to actual execution/publication. Neither source proves the public release time of each January 2025 vintage. The project uses explicit `LASTCHANGED` delay sensitivities and a conservative nominal-run-elapsed guard, not an assertion of measured availability. The original **zero P5MIN coverage of the exact five-minute `t+60` target** is unchanged. The 30-minute predispatch `RRP` was evaluated against a clearly labeled six-interval realised half-hour average, never against an exact five-minute target.
